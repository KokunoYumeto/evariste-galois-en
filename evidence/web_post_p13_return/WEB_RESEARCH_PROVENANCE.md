# Web and literature research provenance

Access date: 2026-08-13.

Every negative conclusion is bounded to the logged corpus. “No prior notice found” is not a novelty claim. A correct form in an earlier witness, a silent normalization in a later transcription, an explicit collation, and a published erratum are classified separately. The inaccessible item-level contents of the 1976 errata list are not inferred from its existence.

## Securely dated or directly supported findings

- **WEB001 / SRC-BOURGNE-AZRA-1976.** The 1976 facsimile printing added a list of errata and two concordance tables; the accessible review does not expose item-level errata. Coordinate: Revue d’histoire des sciences 30(2), p. 179; review paragraphs reporting the new supplementary pages. Source: https://www.persee.fr/doc/rhs_0151-4105_1977_num_30_2_1486
- **WEB002 / SRC-CAUCHY-TERMS.** Cauchy’s système de substitutions conjuguées has been translated as conjoined substitutions; translating it as modern conjugate substitutions is historically hazardous and does not establish pairwise commutativity. Coordinate: Archive for History of Exact Sciences 71, pp. 409–481; footnote 12 in §2.1. Source: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00407-017-0193-8
- **WEB003 / SRC-DICKER-2026.** A modern paper explicitly treats Galois’s incomplete Proposition II and reconstructs missing arguments; it is later scholarship, not an earlier erratum witness. Coordinate: arXiv:2607.20147, abstract and submission history. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20147
- **WEB004 / SRC-NUMDAM-1846.** The publication metadata and linked errata record are securely identified; item-level attribution still requires inspection of the linked errata. Coordinate: JMPA series 1, volume 11, pp. 381–444; record field “corrected-by Errata”. Source: https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1846_1_11__381_0/
- **WEB005 / SRC-ETH-1897.** An independent 1897 comparison copy exists and has IIIF/download infrastructure; the colophon job number was not resolved in the bounded retrieval. Coordinate: Rar 4575; DOI 10.3931/e-rara-19915; catalogue fields for IIIF and downloads. Source: https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/6262819
- **WEB006 / SRC-LOCAL-1908.** A high-resolution 87-page public scan of Tannery’s collation is independently catalogued. Coordinate: File record: 3,735 × 5,802 pixels, 87 pages. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Galois_-_Manuscrits,_édition_Tannery,_1908.djvu
- **WEB007 / SRC-DLMF-LEGENDRE.** The printed 1897/1846 formula EE′+E′K−KK′=π/2, under the source’s F/K notation, has the standard Legendre-relation shape; this does not by itself decode the manuscript double-prime variant. Coordinate: DLMF 19.7.1. Source: https://dlmf.nist.gov/19.7.E1
- **WEB008 / SRC-WIKI-TOC.** The comparison transcription silently gives roman v for the Introduction and supplies a secure latest date for that correction; it is not an explicit erratum notice. Coordinate: Page 91, oldid 7722271. Source: https://fr.wikisource.org/w/index.php?title=Page:Galois_-_Œuvres_mathématiques,_Gauthier-Villars,_1897.djvu/91&oldid=7722271
- **WEB009 / SRC-NEUMANN-2013.** A modern corrected second printing exists, but subscription-limited item pages were not used to attribute specific errata without direct inspection. Coordinate: EMS book overview and contents metadata; DOI 10.4171/104. Source: https://ems.press/books/hem/102
- **WEB010 / SRC-AFFINE-PRIMITIVE.** If V is a faithful irreducible F_pH-module, HV is affine primitive with socle V; this is the modern structural framework used in the degree-16 counterexample and W10 repair. Coordinate: Vietnam Journal of Mathematics 51, introduction around the faithful irreducible module criterion. Source: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10013-023-00607-5
- **WEB011 / SRC-WIKI-1897.** The online comparison copy identifies Harvard College Library provenance and reproduces the corrected Introduction folio v. Coordinate: Book index metadata and complete table of contents. Source: https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Livre:Galois_-_Œuvres_mathématiques,_Gauthier-Villars,_1897.djvu

## Bounded-search limitations

- The ETH independent copy was located, but the high-resolution colophon leaf was not successfully retrieved; W11-U001 therefore remains unresolved.
- The accessible 1977 review establishes that the 1976 edition contains an errata list but does not expose the item-level list. No error is attributed to that list without inspection.
- Subscription-limited pages of Neumann’s corrected second printing were not used for item-level priority claims.
- W08-WV001 remains unresolved because the manuscript double-prime notation could not be decoded with a secure notation key; the standard printed Legendre relation does not justify silently assimilating the manuscript formula.
