### POST-P13-A008 / W08-WV001

Direct replay of the 600 ppi Tannery master establishes the formula

\[
E'F''-E''F'=\frac{\pi}{2}\sqrt{-1},
\]

not the former critical-apparatus transcription beginning with \(F'F''\). Tannery prints this immediately after the complete-integral form and expressly says that Galois wrote Legendre's theorem in the determinant form. DLMF 19.7.1 supplies the modern complete-integral relation, and Beppo Levi's 1927 title supplies the half-period/quasi-period determinant \(\eta_1\omega_2-\eta_2\omega_1=\pi i/2\). The separate proof certificate gives an explicit normalization converting one form into the other. Tannery 1908 is the earliest securely dated explicit notice located.
