## Which reader should I choose?

The **source-faithful English translation** is the uncluttered reading edition. It translates the historical source directly and retains the source-era editorial material and footnotes, but omits modern GPT annotations, correction boxes, editorial IDs, and marker glyphs. A historically defective claim remains translated as printed; it is not silently repaired.

The **GPT-annotated English edition** uses the same translated corpus and adds the modern critical layer: readable corrections, mathematical qualifications, counterexamples, witness differences, and evidence-linked editorial notes.

Both readers cover the complete substantive contents of the 1897 Gauthier-Villars edition, organized as W01–W11 and mapped through 587 aligned English segments. The translated scope includes the front matter, Émile Picard's Introduction, every collected work and fragment, formulas, historical notes, contents, figures, colophon, and intentional page topology.
