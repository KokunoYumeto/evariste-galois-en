## What this edition contains

The reader translates the complete substantive content of Évariste Galois's *Œuvres mathématiques* (Paris: Gauthier-Villars et fils, 1897): the front matter, Émile Picard's Introduction, every collected work and fragment, historical notes, formulas, contents, and colophon. All physical source pages PDF001-PDF096 / leaves L0000-L0095 are accounted for, including figures, excluded scan matter, blank leaves, and page topology. Permanent source-page and segment anchors connect the English text to the audited French diplomatic transcription.

The historical text is translated faithfully even when a printed claim is defective. A nearby editorial note then explains the correction, qualification, counterexample, or witness difference in ordinary prose. Internal evidence identifiers appear only as small editorial references; complete proofs, searches, ledgers, hashes, and provenance remain in the evidence archive.

The completed research program contains 21 concluded tasks: 10 repaired results, 9 proved results, and 2 rejected claims. No task remains unresolved. Three separate preliminary-image questions remain open because the available images do not support a secure reading; they are stated plainly in the reader and evidence.
