\chapter*{Translation and source}
This reader presents a direct English translation of the complete substantive content of the audited French diplomatic transcription of the 1897 Gauthier--Villars edition. It follows the printed historical claims and retains the source-era editorial notes and footnotes. Claims later shown to be defective are not silently corrected here.

Modern correction notes, witness commentary, marker symbols, and internal apparatus codes are intentionally omitted. The companion annotated English edition in the same DOI release supplies that additional context for readers who want it. Invisible source destinations preserve alignment with the fixed French witness without interrupting the reading page.
