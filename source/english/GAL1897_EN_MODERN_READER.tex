\chapter*{Editorial method and scope}
This reader translates the complete substantive content of the audited French diplomatic transcription of the 1897 Gauthier--Villars edition. The main English text follows the printed historical claim, including claims later shown to be defective. A dagger marks a proved or suspected defect; a nearby boxed \emph{GPT Critical Note} gives its stable identifier, correction or qualification, supporting mathematical evidence, transmission status where known, and current research disposition. No correction is silently substituted into Galois's or the historical editors' text.

Source-page markers map the translation to the frozen French witness. The aligned French transcription, source authority, complete ledgers, build scripts, and validation evidence accompany the release. Model and mechanical evidence can close this publication; no human-review gate is asserted.

\chapter*{Post-P13 critical research closure}
Before this exact release, OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol in Pro mode completed the bounded post-P13 critical-research program against the immutable French diplomatic layer. OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol in Ultra mode then integrated the returned results into this English reader and independently rechecked the release. The twenty-one task dispositions are nine \emph{repaired}, eight \emph{proved}, two \emph{rejected}, and two \emph{unresolved after bounded search}. Here \emph{rejected} means that a historical mathematical premise or proposed repair was disproved; it does not mean that the audit failed. The returned cold audit records 242 of 242 checks passing.

\GalCriticalNote{POST-P13-A001}{\textbf{Status: repaired and closed in the critical layer.} All thirty-six proved source-error records now have a proved repair, a qualified replacement, or a counterexample rejecting the printed claim. No historical French byte and no faithfully translated historical claim was silently changed. The item-level certificates are preserved in \texttt{CRITICAL\_ERRATA\_CATALOGUE.csv}.}

\GalCriticalNote{POST-P13-A002}{\textbf{Status: proved and closed.} A machine-readable dependency graph records forty-six local and cross-work edges and classifies each downstream result as surviving, surviving only under added hypotheses, or blocked. The graph accompanies this release as \texttt{DEPENDENCY\_PROPAGATION\_EDGES.csv}.}

\GalCriticalNote{POST-P13-A003}{\textbf{Status: proved to the 2026-08-13 search cutoff.} The prior-notice matrix covers all thirty-six errors and fifteen witness or editorial variants. It distinguishes an earlier correct witness, explicit collation or erratum, later silent normalization, inaccessible candidate errata material, and a bounded negative search. In particular, the accessible record proves that the 1976 Bourgne--Azra printing added an errata list, but the item-level list was unavailable; this edition therefore makes no unsupported attribution to it. See \texttt{BIBLIOGRAPHIC\_PRIOR\_NOTICE\_MATRIX.csv} and \texttt{WEB\_SOURCE\_EVIDENCE.csv}.}

Five records remain openly unresolved: three inherited image readings in the 1897 preliminary matter (\texttt{W01-U001}, \texttt{W01-U002}, and \texttt{W01-U003}); the materially different manuscript double-prime formula at \texttt{W08-WV001}; and the fused printer job number at \texttt{W11-U001}. No reading is supplied where the evidence does not support one. Exact missing evidence is recorded in \texttt{OPEN\_AFTER\_21\_TASKS.csv}.
