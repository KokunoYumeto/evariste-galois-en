\GalCriticalNote{W11-U001}{The printer's number is 24572}{The tiny number remains
fused in this particular scan, but an independent digitization of another 1897
copy clearly shows the five-digit sequence \textbf{24572}. The official ETH
Bibliothek OCR records the same number. The independent image supplies the
decisive reading, while the OCR provides corroboration. The historical colophon
crop is retained above without alteration. This detail has no mathematical or
navigational consequence.}
